Simulation Methodology

The antenna geometries are evaluated using CST Studio Suite before physical fabrication. The simulation is intended to study the electromagnetic response of the proposed structures and identify frequency regions requiring design optimisation.

Parameters of Interest

  • Operating frequency and resonance location
  • S11 reflection coefficient
  • Impedance matching behaviour
  • Antenna geometry and dimensions
  • Frequency shift relative to the project target

Design Iteration

If the simulated resonance does not coincide with the required operating frequency, the antenna geometry and matching parameters can be adjusted and simulated again before fabrication.

Current Development Status

The project is currently at the simulation and design-development stage. Physical prototype fabrication and laboratory measurement have not yet been completed.

Therefore, the values presented here should be interpreted as simulation results rather than experimentally measured antenna performance.

Important: Simulation results are preliminary and may change after geometry optimisation, fabrication, connector integration, mounting on the helmet, and practical RF measurement. Final antenna performance should be established through physical testing.
