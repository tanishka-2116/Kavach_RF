Antenna Design

This section documents the proposed antenna architecture, operating-frequency plan, physical design considerations, materials and dimensional parameters for the KAVACH-RF helmet-mounted conformal antenna concept.

01 — Design Overview

KAVACH-RF Antenna Architecture

KAVACH-RF investigates a low-profile antenna architecture intended for integration with a tactical helmet while maintaining the required RF interface and minimizing external antenna protrusion.

The design work currently considers two operating configurations: UHF and L-band.

Development status: The antenna is currently at the design and simulation stage. Physical prototype fabrication and experimental validation are planned as subsequent steps.
02 — Antenna Architecture

Proposed Physical Stack

The proposed antenna integration consists of multiple layers intended to support conformal mounting and RF performance.

Layer Purpose
Protective / Radome Layer Provides physical protection while allowing electromagnetic transmission.
Conformal Antenna Layer Primary radiating structure.
EBG / AMC Layer Investigated for electromagnetic isolation and radiation-control purposes.
Flexible Dielectric Provides electrical separation and mechanical flexibility.
Helmet Shell Mechanical mounting surface for the integrated antenna structure.

06 — Materials

Material Considerations

Material selection is being considered with respect to electrical performance, mechanical flexibility, fabrication practicality and helmet integration.

  • Conductive radiator / copper structure
  • Flexible dielectric substrate
  • Protective or radome layer
  • Helmet shell as the integration surface
  • RF feed and connector interface
Final material dimensions and dielectric parameters should be confirmed against the selected fabrication material and its manufacturer datasheet before prototype fabrication.
