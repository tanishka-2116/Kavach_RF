03 — Antenna Design

The KAVACH-RF antenna is being developed for integration with a tactical helmet-mounted communication system. The design focuses on a compact radiating structure that can be adapted to the helmet geometry while maintaining the required RF characteristics.

Design Stage — Simulation / Pre-Fabrication
Configuration

Antenna Architecture

The proposed antenna uses a layered structure consisting of a conductive radiating element, dielectric/support material and conductive ground structure. The geometry is intended to support conformal or helmet-mounted implementation.

Construction

Layered Structure

The antenna can be understood as a stack of functional layers. The radiating conductor is separated from the ground/reference conductor by a dielectric or supporting layer.

Exploded Layer View Conceptual view showing the relationship between the antenna layers.
Design note: The final physical dimensions and layer parameters will be refined using electromagnetic simulation and subsequently verified through physical testing after prototype fabrication.

Frequency Development

UHF and L-Band Configurations

The KAVACH-RF development currently considers both UHF and L-band configurations. These configurations are being investigated through electromagnetic modelling before physical fabrication.

Frequency selection, antenna dimensions, matching and radiation characteristics will be refined based on simulation results and subsequent measurements.

Important: The values shown in the project documentation should be treated as development and simulation results unless they have been confirmed through physical measurement.
Next Stage

From Design to Prototype

The antenna design will proceed from electromagnetic simulation to physical fabrication. Prototype measurements will then be used to compare the fabricated antenna with the simulated behaviour.

Important validation parameters include S11, impedance matching, resonant frequency, VSWR, radiation characteristics and practical integration with the helmet structure.
