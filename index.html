01 / Overview

What are we building?

KAVACH-RF investigates a low-profile conformal antenna architecture designed for integration with a helmet surface. The project combines antenna design, electromagnetic simulation and a planned prototype validation workflow.

Conformal Integration

Investigation of an antenna structure that follows the available helmet geometry instead of relying on a conventional protruding antenna.

RF Simulation

CST Studio Suite is being used to investigate resonance, reflection coefficient and electromagnetic behaviour.

Physical Validation

A physical prototype and RF measurement workflow are planned after the antenna geometry is optimized.

03 / Development

From simulation to prototype

The project follows a staged engineering workflow. Simulation results are treated separately from future physical measurements.

01 — Design

Define antenna architecture, materials, geometry and frequency targets.

02 — Simulate

Evaluate electromagnetic behaviour using CST and optimize the antenna geometry.

03 — Validate

Fabricate the optimized design and compare physical RF measurements with simulation.
